What's new

Every release of Actor Tally, newest first. Updates arrive automatically through your browser's store.

4.2 November 2026

The jump from 4.0 includes everything built for 4.1, which never shipped separately.

New

  • Half-star ratings. Click a star once for the whole value, again for a half, a third time to clear. Charts, filters, editors, bulk edit and imports all accept halves.
  • Map timeline. A date slider and play button under the places map replay where your logging happened. Places with no logs yet are dimmed; the latest one carries a ring.
  • Map filter. Replay just one person, one tag, or both.
  • Tag manager. Rename or delete a tag across every log of a page, or tick several and merge them into one. One undo step each.
  • Backup reminder. After a number of new logs or days without an export (15 and 30 by default, whichever comes first), the dashboard offers one. Adjustable, can be snoozed or switched off.
  • Past tags in the popup. Choose how many to show, from none to all.
  • Delete all ratings for a page in one step, with undo.
  • Microsoft Edge Add-ons. Now listed on Edge. Firefox and Opera listings are in review.

Improved

  • More of the interface is translated in all seven languages: chip tooltips in the popup, progress labels under Upcoming charts, alias placeholders and several tooltips.
  • “Rate Actor Tally” and the store row in “Report a bug” open the store you installed from.
  • Exports and image downloads use one routine that works reliably in every browser.
  • The panel drag handle works in Firefox too.

Fixed

  • Milestones counted names instead of logs, so one log with three people moved them three steps. They now count logs, and step back when logs are deleted.
  • With the dashboard open, a log made in the popup didn't show until a reload — and the dashboard's next edit could overwrite it. The dashboard now picks it up immediately.
  • Settings changed in the popup (a running stopwatch, the last place) could be undone by a change on an open dashboard.
  • Tags typed in the popup, the manual form or bulk edit are now stored the same way: lower case, no duplicates on one log.
  • Deleting a page now carries the monthly goal over to the page that takes its number.

4.0 August 2026

  • Insights — a rotating line of plain-language observations about your history.
  • Chart builder — seven measures across fourteen groupings, as a line, bar or area, optionally through your filters; save any chart as an image.
  • Time spent — durations typed or timed with the stopwatch, with totals, a distribution and a monthly trend.
  • Places — label a log with where you were; places with coordinates appear on an offline world map.
  • Co-stars — mark who was billed as a co-star; its own filter, chart grouping and badge.
  • Video position — optionally record how far into a video you were.
  • Add a log manually — for things watched elsewhere, with their own date and time.
  • Everything is editable — every field of every log, from its details window.
  • Per-day breakdown between two dates and a weekday × hour heatmap.
  • Settings drawer — pink theme, banners, panel visibility, drag-to-reorder layout, row limits; monthly goal ring, day streaks, tally marks as an image.
  • Redo alongside twenty steps of undo, and a Report a bug button.
  • Four pages in one install, each with its own word for “actor”.
